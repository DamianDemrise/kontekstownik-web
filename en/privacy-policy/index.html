Privacy policy

Effective from 31 October 2026 (updated: Facebook, Instagram and email channels added). This is a translation; in case of discrepancies the Polish version prevails.

1. Controller

The controller of data collected on www.kontekstownik.pl is Damian Dobroliński. Contact for all data matters: damian@demrise.pl.

2. What we collect

The site has no forms, user accounts or newsletter. You do not give us any personal data.

  • Visit statistics (Google Analytics 4) – only if you click “Accept” in the banner. We collect information about the visit: approximate location (country, city), device and browser type, traffic source, pages viewed and whether the YouTube, TikTok or other channel buttons were clicked. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. Advertising signals and Google Signals are switched off.
  • Your banner choice – stored in your browser (localStorage) so we do not ask on every visit. This information does not reach us.
  • Server logs – the site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any server, it technically processes the visitor’s IP address to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

3. Purpose and legal basis

  • Visit statistics: understanding interest in the site and channels – based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Delivering the site and security: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Recipients and transfers outside the EEA

Statistical data is processed by Google Ireland Ltd as a processor; it may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA on the basis of the European Commission’s decision on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The site is served by GitHub, Inc. (USA), which is also covered by this framework.

6. How long

We keep data in Google Analytics for 14 months; after that it is deleted automatically.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, rectify, erase or restrict its processing, to data portability and to object, and the right to withdraw consent. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland). We do not make automated decisions or profile for advertising purposes.

8. Links to external services and email

The buttons lead to YouTube (Google), TikTok, Facebook and Instagram (Meta), which operate under their own privacy policies. The site does not embed their players, plugins or scripts, so nothing connects to them before you click.

If you write to kontekstownik@gmail.com, we process your address and the content of your message to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and keep them for as long as the correspondence requires. The mailbox is operated by Google.
